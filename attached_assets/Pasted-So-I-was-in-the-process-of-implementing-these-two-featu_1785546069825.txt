Feature 1 Plan — UK Networking Events at Scale
The 900k target: how we get there
Eventbrite's internal /api/v3/destination/search/ endpoint is paginated at 50 results per page, max page ~20, so 1,000 results per query. To hit 900k+ events we need 900+ distinct queries that don't overlap much:

Dimension	Count
UK locations (cities + postcodes + boroughs)	~450
Categories (Business, Tech, Music, Arts, Food, etc.)	20
Keyword sweeps (networking, conference, seminar, hackathon, startup, career, workshop...)	~30
Date windows (rolling 12 months, split quarterly)	4
That's 450 × 20 = 9,000 location/category combos — we won't need all of them, but it gives us massive coverage. In practice 450 locations × 4 date windows × page-through = well over 900k unique events given Eventbrite's UK volume.

Architecture
PostgreSQL (events table, already exists)
     ↑ upsert on eventbriteId
BullMQ Queue: "eventbrite-scrape"
     ↑ jobs added by scheduler
15 Worker Processes (concurrency=15, Redis-backed)
     each worker: fetch 1 page of results → upsert → done
Scheduler (node-cron, already exists):
  - Full sweep (all locations × categories): every 6h
  - Incremental sweep (last 24h events, keyword-only): every 30min
  - Expire cleanup: daily

Why BullMQ over current node-cron approach: BullMQ handles retries, backpressure, deduplication, and lets 15 workers run truly in parallel without blocking the Express server. Current approach is single-threaded sequential — it would take hours to complete a full sweep.

Redis: We'll use Replit's environment or a lightweight in-process Redis emulator (ioredis-mock) for dev, with an env var pointing to a real Redis in production.
